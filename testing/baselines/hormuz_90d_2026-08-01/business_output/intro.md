## Working thesis

This looks like realized, active kinetic escalation rather than latent risk, so near-term price impact is likely driven more by insurance and freight cost inflation plus precautionary self-diversion than by an outright volume shock — unless Iran escalates toward direct mining or blockade attempts. The simple "closed vs. normal" framing likely understates the realistic middle scenario: throughput intact but materially more expensive.
